Three steps, one loop

  1. STEP 1

    Choose one task

    Write it on paper first. A window works best when it has a single, small target.

  2. STEP 2

    Run the window

    Start the timer and stay on the task. If something pops into your head, note it and keep going.

  3. STEP 3

    Take the break

    Stand up, drink water, look away from the screen. The timer switches to rest on its own.

Good to know

Where 25 minutes comes from

The 25-minute work block with a 5-minute break is the Pomodoro Technique, created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. The 50 / 10 and 15 / 3 options suit longer or shorter tasks.

Your count stays on your device

The tally of finished blocks is saved in this browser only and resets each day. Nothing is sent anywhere.
